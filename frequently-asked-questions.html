Booking & Appointments

Do I need an appointment at Amber & Sage Spa?

Yes. We are by appointment only — no walk-ins. This lets us maintain the calm, unrushed environment we're known for.

How do I book an appointment?

The easiest way is online, 24/7. You can also call (423) 218-2841 or text (423) 537-4221 during business hours.

What should I expect on my first visit?

Arrive about 5 minutes early. We'll send you a text when it's time to come inside, so you can decompress in your car first. You'll be greeted with your choice of soothing tea or cold water, settled in a robe, and walked back to a private treatment room. Your therapist or esthetician will personalize the session to what your body or skin needs that day.

Services

What services does Amber & Sage Spa offer?

We're a holistic day spa. Our services include: organic facials (partnered with Éminence Organic Skin Care), therapeutic massage, reiki, sound baths, aura photography using the AuraCloud 3D Pro system, lash and brow services, full-body waxing, and body scrubs.

What kind of facials does Amber & Sage offer?

10 facial options including our 30-minute Express Facial, Personalized Éminence Facial, Dermaplane Facial, Acne Facial, Microdermabrasion, Intraceuticals Oxygen Glow Facial (in 8 variants), Glow and Sculpt Facial, and our 2-hour Buccal Facial Massage. See the decision guide if you're not sure which is right.

Which oxygen facial variant should I pick?

For first-timers with no specific concern, start with Rejuvenate. For aging: Rejuve + Collagen. For dullness: Rejuve + Vitamin C3. For hyperpigmentation: Opulence Brightening. For a major event: GlowTox. See the full Which Oxygen Facial? decision guide.

Does Amber & Sage offer chemical peels?

No — we only offer enzyme peels, not chemical peels. Enzyme peels use plant-derived enzymes from papaya, pumpkin, and pineapple to gently exfoliate without acids. If your skin requires a chemical peel, we'll refer you to a trusted Chattanooga dermatologist or med-spa. Comparison guide.

Does Amber & Sage do couples massage?

We don't offer traditional same-room couples massage. What we do offer is two massages scheduled at the same time in adjoining rooms — you and your partner start and finish together, then meet in the relaxation area afterward. Many regulars actually prefer this format.

Does Amber & Sage do Brazilian waxing?

Yes — Brazilian and bikini waxing are available for female clients. Our full waxing menu also includes brow, lip, chin, underarm, arm, leg, and back waxing.

Does Amber & Sage offer aura photography?

Yes. We use the AuraCloud 3D Pro — the current industry-leading aura imaging technology from Inneractive. Our basic session is about 15 minutes; we also offer an upgraded reading with a detailed personalized PDF report emailed afterward. Pairs and couples sittings are available on request. More on our aura sessions.

What Éminence products does Amber & Sage use?

We are a Proudly Partnered Éminence Organic Skin Care provider. Every facial uses Éminence products, and we retail the full line. Our estheticians are Éminence-certified and receive ongoing brand training.

Gift Cards, Events & Extras

Do Amber & Sage gift cards expire?

Our gift cards are valid for 5 years from the date of purchase. There are no maintenance fees that erode the balance. Cards are available digitally (instantly emailable) or physically from our 24/7 self-serve gift card box outside our entrance.

How can I buy a gift card for Amber & Sage Spa?

Digital gift cards are available online and delivered by email within seconds. Physical gift cards are available 24/7 from the self-serve box outside our front entrance, or book a quick retail appointment to pick one up in person. Full gift card details.

Does Amber & Sage host bachelorette parties or private events?

Yes. We host bachelorette parties, corporate wellness events, milestone birthdays, bridal parties, and custom private groups. Every event is custom-designed rather than pre-packaged. Text (423) 537-4221 with your group size, date range, and occasion to start planning. More on private events.

Is tipping expected at Amber & Sage?

Yes — 20% has become the new standard, mirroring restaurant tipping. For exceptional service or regular therapists you've built a relationship with, 20–25% is common. You can tip directly through our booking system.

Is Amber & Sage the same as Natural Body?

No. Amber & Sage is a separate, independently owned holistic day spa. Our previous Natural Body gift card redemption agreement ended April 30, 2026, and Natural Body cards are no longer accepted.
