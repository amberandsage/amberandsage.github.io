How Couples Massage Works Here

You check in together, settle in with tea or cold water, and head back for your massages at the same time. Each of you has your own licensed massage therapist, and your sessions are timed to finish together, so you walk out side by side, relaxed and in no hurry.

Good to know before you book

We’re a small, intimate spa, so each person is in their own private treatment room rather than sharing one. Same start, same finish, and you’re together the moment you’re done. Plenty of couples tell us they like it this way: no distractions, and each of you gets a session that’s fully about you.

Couples Massage FAQ

Does Amber & Sage Spa offer couples massage in Chattanooga?

Yes. We book couples massages so two people can arrive, relax, and leave together. Each person has their own licensed massage therapist in a private treatment room, and both sessions start and finish at the same time.

Are we in the same room for a couples massage?

No. Our space is intimate, so each of you has your own private treatment room. Your massages are scheduled to start and finish at the same time, so you check in together and meet up again right after. Many couples like that each person gets a quiet, fully personalized session.

How do I book a couples massage?

Book online and choose couples massage — the booking system schedules both massages at the same time. You can also call or text (423) 250-7243 and we’ll set it up for you. For your preferred weekend day and time, booking a week or two ahead is ideal, but we’re usually pretty good at fitting couples in on shorter notice, so it’s always worth checking.

Can we each choose a different type of massage?

Yes. Because each of you has your own therapist and room, one person can book deep tissue while the other books Swedish, hot stone, or prenatal massage. Just keep both sessions the same length so you finish together.

Can I buy a couples massage as a gift?

Yes. Our digital gift cards are delivered instantly by email and can be used for two massages booked together. They make an easy anniversary, Valentine’s Day, wedding, or holiday gift.

Can we get a same-day couples massage?

Often, yes. Check online booking for today’s couples openings, or call or text (423) 250-7243 and we’ll do our best to fit you in.
